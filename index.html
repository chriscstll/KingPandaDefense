Our Services

  • Ballistic Glass Replacement

    Factory glass replaced with multi-layered ballistic-resistant glazing. Optically clear, rated to B6/B7, and fitted to your vehicle's exact window geometry without altering its appearance.

  • Body & Door Armoring

    Doors, pillars, floor, and roof lined with ballistic steel or composite panels. Full cabin coverage fitted around your vehicle's original frame — no structural compromise, no visible change.

  • Run-Flat Tire System

    Internal run-flat inserts fitted to your existing wheels. Maintains full vehicle control and mobility for up to 80km after complete tire failure — no spare required.

  • Suspension & Brake Upgrade

    Reinforced suspension and upgraded brake systems calibrated to handle the added weight of armor without affecting ride comfort or handling characteristics your client expects.

  • Discreet Add-On Systems

    Optional secondary systems fitted on request — intercom, emergency lighting, GPS tracking, and interior emergency exits. Integrated cleanly so the vehicle reads as civilian at all times.

  • Certification & Documentation

    Every build leaves with a full certification report — protection level achieved, materials used, and test results. Required for insurance, government compliance, and border documentation.
